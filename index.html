Footnote Sequencer

Add footnotes in the order they appear in your essay. The sequencer gives the full citation the first time a source appears, Ibid when the same source is repeated in the very next footnote, and a cross-reference such as (n 3) after that.

For cases, put judicial officers in brackets after the pinpoint.

    Bibliography

    Built from your saved sources and grouped into AGLC sections. The first author’s name is inverted, entries are alphabetised (ignoring “The”) and there are no closing full stops. Empty sections are dropped and the letters re-run.
